One mad doctor. One missing girlfriend. One questionable plan.

In the original story, a meteor never landed in the Addisons' garden. Dr. Ed still managed to go completely off the rails, and now he's kidnapped Sandy.

Jack has enjoyed bachelor life for a while, but apparently there's a limit. You're Jack: round up two friends, find a way into the mansion, and discover what the Doc is building in the basement. Poke at everything, talk to everyone, collect suspicious objects, and try ideas that probably shouldn't work. Clever thinking earns points. So does surviving your own curiosity.

Point. Click. Question your decisions.

This is a point-and-click adventure, so naturally the solution may involve clicking absolutely everything. Left-click to walk, use objects, pick things up or talk to people. Right-click to look first: the descriptions are often worth the detour.

Mouse & keyboard: the classic setup

  • Left click: walk, use, talk, pick up
  • Right click: look at something
  • Tab: inventory
  • H (hold): show everything you can click
  • F5 / F7: save / load
  • Esc: skip a cutscene · Shift: faster speech
  • F12: options (language, speed, fullscreen and more)
  • F11: fullscreen

Gamepad: couch detective mode

  • Sticks: move the pointer (use both to go faster)
  • D-pad: walk
  • A / R1: use · B / L1: look
  • X: skip scene · Y: skip text
  • Back: show hotspots · Start: options
  • R2 / L2: save / load

Touch: tap your way into trouble

On phones and tablets, tap to walk and use things. The buttons next to the picture switch between USE and LOOK and give you Save, Load, Options, a Hint, Skip Scene and Skip Text. Turn your phone sideways and the buttons move to the side. If you leave the app, your game is kept and waiting for you when you come back.

Stuck? The mansion is winning. Hold H (or tap Hint) to reveal what you might have missed. If the Doc still has you stumped, consult the original complete walkthrough (in German).

A love letter to the golden age of clicking everything

Mänick Mänschion is Thomas Holz's lovingly handmade nod to Maniac Mansion, written, drawn and released for free in the mid-1990s. It runs on his own adventure engine, MOAS. This edition brings the original game to modern computers, phones and handhelds, with extra languages plus touch and gamepad support. The story and puzzles remain gloriously old-school.

Curious about the original? Visit Thomas Holz's release page. The game's graphics are also available as public domain game resources.
