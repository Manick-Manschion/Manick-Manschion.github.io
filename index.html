About

Mänick Mänschion is a German point-and-click adventure from the mid-1990s by Thomas Holz — a humorous, home-made parody of Maniac Mansion. It runs on the MOAS (Mouse Orientiertes Adventure System) engine, originally written for DOS (VGA Mode 13h, SoundBlaster). This site hosts the modern SDL2 port: same game logic, platform layer for video, input, timer and audio, with ports to Linux, Windows, Android, WebAssembly, R36S and a DOS rebuild, and translations into five languages.

Original release page: thomasholz.de/manick.htm.

Controls

  • Left click / tap — walk, use, talk, pick up
  • Right click — look at an object (on touch: the LOOK mode button)
  • Tab — inventory bar
  • F5 / F7 — save / load
  • Esc — skip a cutscene (when the game allows it); Shift — speed up speech
  • H (hold) — outline everything you can click in the room
  • F12 — options: resume, save, load, restart, quit, and settings (language, fullscreen, touch buttons, game and speech speed; remembered for the next start)
  • F11 or Alt+Enter — fullscreen; Alt+Q — quit
  • Gamepad — left stick or D-pad walk, right stick cursor, RT use, LT look, A skip, B/Start Esc, Back options

On Android and in the browser, touch buttons provide Save, Load, USE / LOOK mode, Options, Hint, Skip Scene and Skip Text, so the classic mouse workflow works without a pointer. In landscape they sit beside the picture; in portrait the picture takes the full width and the buttons move underneath. Save and Load are greyed out while the game would ignore them (cutscenes, dialogues). Progress is kept automatically when you leave the app and restored on the next start.
